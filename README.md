# Kartikeya Gangwar

I am an undergraduate studying mathematics at the University of Delhi. *(Official / Academic Documents: Kartikey Singh)*.

I don’t care much for titles, academic politics, or conventional career ladders. I am a backbencher who fell in love with hard, beautiful problems—because when I came to the city for college and found the routine empty, I chose to spend my time building genuine capability rather than collecting credentials.

Most of my days are split between different kinds of physical and intellectual reality:
- **Mathematics & Computation:** Symplectic geometry, high-dimensional stochastic PDEs, fluid dynamics, and designing custom neural autodiff engines from scratch.
- **Physical Grit:** Running, farming in my village, tractor mechanics, and midnight burpees. If the body isn't tempered like iron, the mind becomes fragile.
- **Craft & Hands-on Work:** Sketching with pencil on paper, understanding mechanical systems, and writing bare-metal open-source code that actually runs fast.

I believe in **open science**: if your mathematics is sound and your code runs, you have nothing to hide and no gatekeeper to fear.

### Selected Research & Systems

- **[eit-neural-surrogate-inversion](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/eit-neural-surrogate-inversion)** — Derivative-informed B-spline neural surrogates for zero-online-FEM shape reconstruction in Electrical Impedance Tomography *(Under review at IEEE Transactions on Computational Imaging)*.
- **[pinn-fluid-formulations](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/pinn-fluid-formulations)** — Investigating operator conditioning and false convergence in incompressible Navier–Stokes PINNs *(Under review at Physics of Fluids)*.
- **[lid-driven-cavity-cfd](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/lid-driven-cavity-cfd)** — Exact spectral Poisson solver ($\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$) and continuation up to $Re = 100{,}000$ on $1025^2$ grids *(Under review at Applied Mathematical Modelling)*.
- **[cpa-shnn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/cpa-shnn)** — Symplectic Hamiltonian neural networks preserving exact energy invariants across chaotic multi-body celestial systems.
- **[as-pinn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/as-pinn)** & **[null-space-pinn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/null-space-pinn)** — Decoupling gradient conflict through orthogonal direct-sum parameter manifolds and autonomous parameter-space AMR.
- **[Deep-EEP-PINN](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/Deep-EEP-PINN)** — Pricing 50-dimensional American basket options via directional autograd Hessian trace contraction in $\mathcal{O}(d)$ linear complexity.
- **[BRSDK](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/BRSDK)** — Real-time telemetry framework operating in BeamNG's 2,000 Hz physics thread with zero dynamic memory allocation and Apache Arrow integration.
