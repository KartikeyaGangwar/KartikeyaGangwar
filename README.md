Undergraduate Researcher in Mathematics &bull; University of Delhi

I work from first principles at the intersection of **computational mathematics**, **scientific machine learning**, and **high-performance systems**.

I build from first principles—confronting difficult, high-dimensional problems by engineering the exact mathematical machinery and bare-metal software required to understand and master them.

My work and daily practice are anchored in three disciplines:
- **Mathematical Invariants:** Symplectic geometry, infinite-dimensional operator conditioning, high-dimensional stochastic PDEs, and writing custom autograd engines from scratch.
- **Physical Discipline:** Grounded in my village roots through farming, operating heavy machinery, and relentless daily athletic conditioning. A sharp intellect requires a resilient physical foundation.
- **The Visual Craft:** Sketching with pencil on paper. Drawing trained my intuition for continuous curves, contours, and spatial balance long before I formalized them into B-splines and Riemannian manifolds.

I practice **open science**: every theoretical formulation and computational engine I build is published openly with complete reproducibility and persistent Zenodo DOIs, inviting rigorous verification and real-world extension.

### Selected Research Frameworks

- **[eit-neural-surrogate-inversion](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/eit-neural-surrogate-inversion)** — Derivative-informed neural surrogates for zero-online-FEM shape inversion in Electrical Impedance Tomography *(Under review at IEEE Transactions on Computational Imaging)*.
- **[pinn-fluid-formulations](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/pinn-fluid-formulations)** — Operator conditioning and false convergence in incompressible Navier–Stokes PINNs *(Under review at Physics of Fluids)*.
- **[lid-driven-cavity-cfd](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/lid-driven-cavity-cfd)** — Exact $\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$ Discrete Sine Transform Poisson solver and steady continuation up to $Re = 100{,}000$ on $1025^2$ grids *(Under review at Applied Mathematical Modelling)*.
- **[cpa-shnn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/cpa-shnn)** — Symplectic Hamiltonian neural networks preserving exact energy invariants across chaotic multi-body celestial systems.
- **[as-pinn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/as-pinn)** & **[null-space-pinn](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/null-space-pinn)** — Decoupling gradient interference via direct-sum parameter manifolds and autonomous parameter-space AMR.
- **[Deep-EEP-PINN](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/Deep-EEP-PINN)** — Pricing 50-dimensional American basket options via directional autograd trace contraction in $\mathcal{O}(d)$ linear complexity.
- **[BRSDK](https://github.com/KartikeyaGangwar/BRSDK)** — Real-time telemetry framework operating inside BeamNG's 2,000 Hz physics thread with zero dynamic Lua allocations and Apache Arrow integration.
